## 🎯 What This Is

A growth proposal that moves Cardano adoption **out of the screen and into the physical world** using QR codes, a children's mini-game, and a parent education path.

**One scan, two audiences served at once:**

| Audience | What they get | What we get |
|---|---|---|
| **Children (6–14)** | A free mini-game and their first NFT | Emotional hook, word-of-mouth |
| **Parents** | A plain-language explainer and an optional first wallet | Qualified, engaged users |

ADA and NFT minting are the **medium**, not the sales pitch — which is both safer under South African regulation and more effective at producing long-term users.

---

## 🔁 The Core Loop

**Physical placements:** T-shirts, wall posters, table cards, event booths. Vehicle magnets are **low priority**; LED/light-box vehicle advertising is **avoided** (South African enforcement risk).

**QR call-to-action:**

> **Scan Me**
> **Let your child create their first NFT — free**

*(Not "Build a future with us, Cardano, changing the world" — that's a brand slogan, not a reason to scan.)*

---

## 💡 Why It Works

> **Children pull parents in — not the other way around.**

- Kids engage with **play and creation**.
- Parents engage with **understanding and safety**.
- A **family experience converts better than a solo pitch**.

Instead of selling ADA to a skeptical adult, we let a child create something real and free. The parent's natural question — *"what just happened?"* — becomes our conversion moment.

## ⚖️ Compliance Summary

- **South Africa:** crypto assets are financial products under FAIS, regulated by the **FSCA**.
- **ARB Code of Advertising Practice:** crypto ads must state investment may result in loss of principal; must not exploit inexperience; must not promise returns.
- **Language rule:** say *learn, create, experience, own* — **never** *earn, returns, easy, fast*.
- **All financial-product materials carry a risk warning.**

> Safer language is not just legally required — it attracts better, longer-term users and protects the brand.

**Full checklist:** [`compliance.md`](compliance.md)

## 📊 Success Metrics

We track the funnel — **not vanity metrics**.

| Metric | What it tells us |
|---|---|
| Game completion rate | Does the child experience hold attention? |
| NFT mint rate | Does play convert to ownership? |
| Parent-path entry rate | Does the child pull the parent in? |
| Wallet creation rate | Does education convert to action? |
| 7-day retention | Do users come back? |
| Revenue per completed loop | Is the model commercially viable? |

*Scan count and follower count are diagnostic only — never the goal.*
