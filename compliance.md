# Compliance Checklist — Cardano Family QR Experience

> **Read this before any marketing material goes live.**
> This document is a working checklist, not legal advice. Consult a South African
> financial-services lawyer before launch.

---

## 1. Regulatory Context (South Africa)

| Regulator / Framework | What it covers | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| **FSCA** (Financial Sector Conduct Authority) | Crypto assets are financial products under FAIS | Any promotion of ADA as an investment may require licensing |
| **FAIS** (Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act) | Advice and intermediary services | Giving "advice" on ADA without a licence is prohibited |
| **ARB Code of Advertising Practice** | All advertising content | Crypto ads must not mislead or promise returns |
| **POPIA** (Protection of Personal Information Act) | Personal data | Children's data requires extra care; parent-gated accounts |
| **National Road Traffic Act + municipal by-laws** | Vehicle advertising | LED/light-box vehicle ads have been enforced against (e.g. Cape Town / Bolt) |
| **Google Ads policy** | Paid advertising | Crypto exchange/wallet ads targeting SA require FSCA registration |

## 2. Language Rules (Hard Rules)

### ✅ Approved words
> learn · create · experience · own · play · explore · discover · family · free (where true)

### ❌ Prohibited words
> earn · returns · profit · easy · fast · guaranteed · passive income · invest now · get rich · financial freedom · double your money

### Rule of thumb
If a sentence could be read as **"you will make money,"** rewrite it.

## 3. Pre-Launch Checklist

### 3.1 Messaging
- [ ] Every QR call-to-action reviewed against Section 2
- [ ] Landing page header contains no return promise
- [ ] Parent explainer reviewed for plain language (no jargon)
- [ ] No screenshot of ADA price charts used as a hook
- [ ] No testimonial implying profit

### 3.2 Risk Warning
- [ ] Risk warning present on any page referencing ADA as a financial product
- [ ] Risk warning text approved (see Section 5)
- [ ] Risk warning legible (not buried in footer small print)

### 3.3 Children's Experience
- [ ] No personal data collected from children
- [ ] Parent-gated account creation (if any account exists)
- [ ] NFT described as "proof of participation," not an asset to sell
- [ ] No in-game purchases without parent confirmation
- [ ] Age-appropriate content review completed

### 3.4 Wallet / ADA Path
- [ ] Wallet creation is **optional**, clearly labelled
- [ ] The 2–5 ADA gift is framed as "to use," not "to hold"
- [ ] No advice given on what to do with ADA beyond the guided first use
- [ ] If any investment framing is added, FSCA licensing status confirmed first

### 3.5 Physical Placements
- [ ] No LED / light-box / large vehicle wraps
- [ ] Car magnets are small, static, on private vehicles only
- [ ] Insurance and lease terms checked before vehicle use
- [ ] Posters/table cards placed where scanning is safe (not while driving)

### 3.6 Advertising Channels
- [ ] If running Google/Meta ads targeting SA: FSCA registration confirmed
- [ ] Influencer/ambassador agreements include language rules
- [ ] All paid placements reviewed against ARB Code

### 3.7 Data & Privacy
- [ ] POPIA compliance review completed
- [ ] Privacy policy published and linked
- [ ] Data-retention policy defined for pilot data

## 4. Incident Response

If a complaint or regulatory query arrives:

1. **Pause** the affected material immediately.
2. **Log** the complaint (date, channel, content, complainant).
3. **Review** against this checklist.
4. **Correct or withdraw** within 48 hours.
5. **Consult** legal counsel before resuming.
6. **Record** the outcome in `pilot/results.md`.

---

## 5. Approved Risk Warning Text

Use verbatim where a financial product is referenced:

> **Risk warning:** Crypto assets are volatile and may result in loss of principal.
> This experience is educational and is not investment advice. You may lose the
> full value of any crypto assets you acquire. Only participate with funds you
> can afford to lose.
