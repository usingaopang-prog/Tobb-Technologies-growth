# Messaging Library — Approved Copy

> **This is the only approved language for the Cardano Family QR Experience.**
> If a phrase is not in this document, it is not approved.
> When in doubt, ask the compliance reviewer before publishing.

**Related:** [Compliance checklist](compliance.md) · [Metrics](metrics.md) · [Overview](overview.md)

---

## 1. The One Rule

> **Say what people can *do*. Never say what they will *earn*.**

Every approved phrase describes an **action** (scan, play, create, learn, own) or an **experience** (fun, free, safe, family). No approved phrase describes a **financial outcome** (profit, return, income, growth).

---

## 2. Approved vs. Prohibited Words

### ✅ Approved words
> learn · create · experience · own · play · explore · discover · family · free (where true) · build · try · start · understand · safe · simple · first

### ❌ Prohibited words
> earn · returns · profit · easy · fast · guaranteed · passive income · invest now · get rich · financial freedom · double your money · moon · pump · opportunity (financial sense) · income · yield · ROI

### ⚠️ Use with caution (only with compliance sign-off)
> invest · ADA · wallet · blockchain · crypto · NFT · Cardano

These are **neutral** terms — they describe the technology, not an outcome. They are fine to use, but never in a sentence that implies financial gain.

---

## 3. The Test

Before publishing any sentence, apply this test:

1. **Could a reasonable person read this as "I will make money"?** If yes → rewrite.
2. **Does it describe an action or an experience?** If no → rewrite.
3. **Would it survive being screenshotted and shown to a regulator?** If no → rewrite.

## 4. QR Call-to-Action Copy

| Placement | Approved copy | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **T-shirt (parent-facing)** | `Scan Me · Let your child create their first NFT — free` | Concrete, action-linked, matches landing page |
| **Wall poster** | `Scan Me · Let your child create their first NFT — free` | Same as T-shirt |
| **Table card** | `Scan Me · Let your child create their first NFT — free` | Same |
| **Event booth** | `Scan Me · Build your child's first blockchain creation` | Slightly softer, event-appropriate |
| **Car magnet (small)** | `Scan Me · Free kids NFT` | Shortest version — must be readable at a glance |

### ❌ Rejected QR copy
| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| `Build a future with us, Cardano, changing the world` | Too long, too abstract, mismatched to landing page |
| `Earn with Cardano — scan now` | Prohibited word "earn" |
| `Easy money with ADA` | Prohibited words "easy" + "money" |
| `Invest in your child's future` | Implies financial product |
| `Passive income on Cardano` | Prohibited phrase |

## 9. Risk Warning (Approved Text — Use Verbatim)

Use this exact text anywhere a financial product is referenced:

> **Risk warning:** Crypto assets are volatile and may result in loss of principal. This experience is educational and is not investment advice. You may lose the full value of any crypto assets you acquire. Only participate with funds you can afford to lose.

Do not paraphrase. Do not shorten. Do not move it into footer small print.

## 10. Tone of Voice

| Attribute | How it sounds |
|---|---|
| **Warm** | Speaks to families, not traders |
| **Clear** | No jargon; if a 10-year-old can't follow it, rewrite |
| **Honest** | Never overpromises; never implies returns |
| **Playful** | Light, curious, encouraging — never hype |
| **Safe** | Reassuring to parents; protective of children |

### Voice examples

| Instead of… | Say… |
|---|---|
| "Unlock the power of blockchain" | "Create something that belongs to you" |
| "Join the financial revolution" | "Try your first digital collectible" |
| "Maximize your ADA" | "Use a small amount of ADA to try it" |
| "The future of money" | "A new way to own things online" |
| "Don't miss out" | "Have a look — it's free" |

## 11. Common Questions (Approved Answers)

**Q: Is this safe for my child?**
> Yes. No personal data is collected from children. The game is web-based, requires no download, and the collectible is created without any payment or wallet setup by the child.

**Q: Do I have to buy anything?**
> No. The first collectible is free. Creating a wallet and receiving a small amount of ADA is entirely optional.

**Q: Will we make money?**
> This experience is educational. It is not an investment, and we do not promise any financial return. Crypto assets are volatile and you may lose the full value of anything you acquire.

**Q: What is an NFT?**
> A digital collectible that is created once and owned by one person. In this experience, it's a proof of participation — something your child made and owns.

**Q: What is Cardano?**
> A blockchain — a public record that lets people create and own digital things without a middleman. It's the technology behind your child's collectible.
